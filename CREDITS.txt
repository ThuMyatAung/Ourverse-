Ourverse — Cosmic Observatory 3D (upgraded build)
================================================

TEXTURES (in ./textures/)
Planet, Sun, Moon and Saturn-ring textures by Solar System Scope
(https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), distributed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
derived from NASA elevation and imagery data.
Note: the Ceres, Haumea, Makemake and Eris maps are Solar System
Scope's "fictional" artistic renderings (no complete real surface
maps of those bodies exist). Pluto keeps its solid colour — no
suitable free equirectangular map was available in these sets.
Asteroids without a photo map use a procedurally generated rocky
surface tinted with each body's colour.

POSITIONS
Planetary elements: NASA/JPL approximate Keplerian elements
(valid 1800–2050). Asteroid elements: NASA/JPL Small-Body
Database (SBDB) osculating elements, epoch JD 2461200.5
(Bennu: JD 2455562.5). Moon: simplified lunar theory.
Positions verified against Swiss Ephemeris (max longitude error
under 0.5 degrees across 1990–2030 in the J2000 frame).
Satellite markers (ISS, Hubble) are schematic orbits, not live
TLE tracking; JWST rides the Sun–Earth L2 point with a halo wobble.

SOUND
Per-planet tones use Hans Cousto's "Cosmic Octave" planetary
frequencies, synthesized live with WebAudio (no audio files).
